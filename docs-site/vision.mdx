---
title: "Vision and development path"
description: "Reusable laboratory evaluations and partnerships that strengthen defenses against biological misuse."
---

Inspect Labs aims to become shared evaluation infrastructure for AI-operated
laboratories. Researchers should be able to measure what scientific agents can
accomplish, check laboratory outcomes and test defenses across software, laboratory
services and robots. Biological misuse prevention is our first priority.

The framework, environments and research partnerships serve different roles:

| Layer | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Inspect Labs | Reliable interfaces and evaluation methods that researchers can reuse |
| Litmus Labs | Domain-reviewed environments representing meaningful laboratory work |
| Litmus partnerships | Scientific judgment, authorized access, recurring studies and verification of fixes |

## Studies we aim to support

| Study | What it could establish |
| --- | --- |
| Hybrid evaluations with wet-lab components | Performance and safeguards across digital and physical work |
| Experimental verification | Whether model-generated outputs work experimentally |
| Pilot uplift studies | Task feasibility and measures for larger randomized studies |
| Rapid uplift studies | Human performance with and without AI on bounded research tasks |
| Demonstrations for decision makers | Checked capabilities and failures relevant to safeguards |

These are proposed uses. Researchers define assignment, comparison conditions,
validated measurements and analysis. The framework supplies connections and evidence,
not causal inference or scientific validity by itself.

## What would make this useful

The next research milestone is an independently useful environment that another
researcher can adopt for a study. It needs meaningful tasks, independently checked
outcomes, difficult legitimate controls and reproducible records.

A candidate spans research planning, provider review, simulated robotic execution
and result analysis using harmless materials. Choose the exact workflow with
prospective research users. Test what agents can accomplish, whether safeguards
prevent unauthorized work while allowing legitimate research, and whether
intervention stops downstream execution. Verify the resulting state rather than
treating a hold or cancellation receipt as prevention.

Robotics is central to the physical laboratory mission. Robot actions affect samples
and equipment, and evaluations need to connect those actions to software decisions,
service activity and laboratory measurements.

Provider partnerships can turn findings into safeguards and active defenses, then
repeat the evaluation after changes. A verified fix and repeat research use are
stronger evidence of usefulness than an increasing adapter count.

## Development gates

1. **Software foundation.** Installed task authoring, native execution and saved-evidence
   replay work outside the checkout. Failures and missing observations remain distinct.
2. **Reusable research environment.** Domain review and an independent author establish
   that tasks and observations serve a meaningful study.
3. **Robotic laboratory simulation.** A real robot policy has repeated native trials,
   independently checked outcomes and separate execution and safeguard measures.
4. **Hybrid study.** An approved protocol connects digital or robotic steps to validated
   experimental measurements and appropriate comparisons.
5. **Recurring provider evaluation.** Authorized access and provider-specific policies
   support observed outcomes and repeat verification of fixes.

Environment-building challenges could recruit contributors once a reliable starter
environment and tested judging method exist. Incident-response exercises could test
detection, intervention, downstream containment and recovery with specialist partners.
These are future directions, not deployed services.

The immediate invitation is to develop meaningful tasks, review their observations,
and independently use the framework. Domain experts, evaluation researchers,
roboticists and infrastructure providers all have concrete contributions to make.
