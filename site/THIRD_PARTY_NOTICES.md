# Site dependencies

The site is rendered by [Quarto](https://quarto.org/) (MIT). Quarto copies its own
front-end libraries, including Bootstrap (MIT), Bootstrap Icons (MIT), clipboard.js
(MIT) and its search components, into `_site/site_libs/` with their notices.

The site self-hosts Inter and IBM Plex Mono from Fontsource 5.3.0. Both fonts use
the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the license texts are in `assets/fonts/`.

These licenses govern their respective components. Original site content uses the
repository's MIT license. The paper is a submitted manuscript; the software license
does not grant a separate permission to republish it.
